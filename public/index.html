The format

How Knockdown Fantasy works

Season-long or dynasty. Snake or auction draft. Up to 8 managers per league.

01

Draft your roster

Snake or auction format. Build a 20-fighter roster from 233 active athletes across every weight class. Champions, top contenders, and deep-cut prospects — your call.

02

Set your lineup

Pick 3 starters before the card locks Saturday night. Lineup lock hits at the first prelim. After that, you ride whatever happens inside the cage.

03

Score on fight night

Points for sig strikes, takedowns, control time, and finishes. A first-round KO on the main card scores more than a five-round decision on the prelims. Finishing is always the right call.

The rulebook

How scoring works

Points for every significant action inside the cage. The faster the finish, the bigger the bonus. Main event fighters score 20% more than the undercard.
