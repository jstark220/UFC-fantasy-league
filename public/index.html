The format

How Knockdown Fantasy works

Season-long or dynasty. Snake or auction draft. 4–14 managers per league.

01

Draft your roster

Snake draft with a live cinematic room — pick clock, board reactions, presence dots, and a queue you can build in advance. Mock the draft first if you want practice. Run auto-draft if you can't make it.

02

Set your lineup

Pick 2 starters before the card locks Saturday night. Lineup lock hits at the first prelim. After that, you ride whatever happens inside the cage.

03

Score on fight night

Points for sig strikes, takedowns, control time, and finishes — live, between rounds. A first-round KO on the main card scores more than a five-round decision on the prelims. Finishing is always the right call.

The rulebook

How scoring works

Points for every significant action inside the cage. The faster the finish, the bigger the bonus. Main event fighters score 20% more than the undercard.

Base scoring

Significant strike landed +0.1
Takedown +1
Knockdown +2
Control time (per second) +0.01

Win bonuses

Finish in Round 1 +18
Finish in Round 2 +14
Finish in Round 3 +9
Finish in Round 4 or 5 +8
Decision win +6
KO inside 60 seconds +5 extra

Title & opponent bonuses

Divisional title win or defense +12
BMF / interim title win or defense +8
Defeated top-5 opponent +8
Defeated top-10 opponent +5
Defeated top-15 opponent +3

Title and opponent-rank bonuses don't stack — a winner takes whichever is highest, not both.

Performance & multipliers

Fight of the Night +4
Draw (both fighters) +3
Main event fighter 1.2×
Co-main event fighter 1.1×

Weekly schedule

Thursday Pre-event waiver window opens. Roster cap expands by +3 for the Temporary Extended Roster Flex (TERF).
Friday Pre-event waivers process in reverse-standings order. Successful claimants move to the back of the queue.
Saturday Lineup locks at the first prelim. Live scoring kicks in. Watch your points tick up between rounds.
Sunday Post-event waiver window opens. Roster cap reverts to base.
Tuesday Post-event waivers process. Drop-down deadline for the +3 TERF.
Wednesday Auto-drop sweep — most-recent acquisitions get released until rosters are back at the base cap.
